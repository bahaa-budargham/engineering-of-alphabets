Α α (Alpha) – /a/

Β β (Beta) – /v/

Γ γ (Gamma) – /ɣ/ (before /a/, /o/, /u/) or /ʝ/ (before /e/, /i/)

Δ δ (Delta) – /ð/

Ε ε (Epsilon) – /e/

Ζ ζ (Zeta) – /z/

Η η (Eta) – /i/

Θ θ (Theta) – /θ/

Ι ι (Iota) – /i/ or /j/

Κ κ (Kappa) – /k/ (before /a/, /o/, /u/) or /c/ (before /e/, /i/)

Λ λ (Lambda) – /l/

Μ μ (Mu) – /m/

Ν ν (Nu) – /n/

Ξ ξ (Xi) – /ks/

Ο ο (Omicron) – /o/

Π π (Pi) – /p/

Ρ ρ (Rho) – /r/ (tap/trill)

Σ σ/ς (Sigma) – /s/ (voiced to /z/ before voiced consonants)

Τ τ (Tau) – /t/

Υ υ (Upsilon) – /i/ (or /f//v/ in digraphs αυ/ευ)

Φ φ (Phi) – /f/

Χ χ (Chi) – /x/ (before /a/, /o/, /u/) or /ç/ (before /e/, /i/)

Ψ ψ (Psi) – /ps/

Ω ω (Omega) – /o/
